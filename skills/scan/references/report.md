# Prospect report template

Write it in the site's language, for the business owner, not a developer. One page: cut before
you add. Fill every `<…>`; drop a section only when the scan skipped it (say "not measured").

```markdown
# Findability check · <company>

<date> · <url>

## In one sentence

<Where they stand, plainly: "Google can read your site well, but you are not named when people
ask for <service> in <region>.">

## What already works

1. <a strength, with the fact behind it>
2. <…>
3. <…>

## Biggest opportunities

1. <what is missing> — <what it costs: customers who search for X land at a competitor>
2. <…>
3. <…>

## What AI assistants say

Asked 3 questions a customer would ask. <Company> was named in <n> of 3. Named instead:
<competitors>.

## Next step

A keyword and page plan: which words your customers use, which pages answer them, and what to
improve first. <one line on how to reach the sender>

---

Measured on <date>: <n> pages (technique), search data <source | not available>, Google Maps
listing, 3 AI questions. A snapshot, not a guarantee of positions.
```
