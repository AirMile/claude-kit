# Intake questions

The core set fits every business; add 1-3 variants for its type. These are starting points:
rewrite each one with what the site and the competitors showed, and drop what is already known.

## Core set

**Goal**

1. What should the site bring in: quote requests, calls, orders, job applicants? Which counts most?
2. What is a good lead for you, and what is a bad one?

**Customers**

3. Who searches for you, and who decides? (role, sector, private or business)
4. Which questions do you hear again and again by phone or email?
5. Which words do customers use for what you do? Often not the trade term.

**Offer**

6. Which 3 services or products do you most want to sell (margin, capacity)?
7. Where do you work: which region, countries, languages? Where not?
8. Which 3 competitors do you run into online?

**Proof**

9. What shows you're good at it: certificates, standards, cases, reviews, numbers?
10. Who checks technical or legal claims before they go online?

**Access**

11. Access to Search Console, Analytics and the Google Business Profile, and when moving from an
    old site: its address, so its pages can redirect.

## Variants

**Local service**

- In which places do you really work, not just "the region"?
- Is there a Google Business Profile, and who manages it?
- Do you ask customers for a review, and how?

**B2B niche**

- Which sectors and applications matter most?
- Which standards or certifications do buyers ask about?
- What do buyers want to read before they call: datasheets, manuals, cases?
- Do buyers in other countries read your site, and in which language?

**Webshop**

- Which categories carry the margin?
- What do buyers compare before they order: specs, delivery, price?
- Which products are hard to find on your site now?
